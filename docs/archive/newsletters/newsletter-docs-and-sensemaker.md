## It just got easier to find your way around SpiritDAO

Our ecosystem has grown a lot — pods, governance, the marketplace, events, the
$SELF and $SYSTEM economy. This update is about making all of it easier to
understand, whether you're brand new or have been here since the beginning.

Two things changed.

### Sensemaker now knows the whole platform

**Sensemaker** is our AI guide. Until now, the version on our website could only
talk about our philosophy — Temporal Naturalism and the Eight Dignities. Ask it
"how do I join a pod?" and it came up short.

Not anymore. Sensemaker now answers questions about **how the platform actually
works** — pods, governance, tokens, events, the marketplace — *and* the
philosophy behind it, all in one place. And its knowledge has been brought fully
up to date, so it describes SpiritDAO as it is today, not as it was months ago.

Ask it anything: *"What is $SELF?"* · *"How do I create a proposal?"* ·
*"What's Tenure?"* · *"How do donations work?"*

### A real documentation library

We've published a proper set of **docs** — clear, organized guides to every part
of the platform. Whether you want a quick "how do I do X" or the full picture of
how SpiritDAO is built, it's now written down and easy to browse, with search.

Everything in the docs and everything Sensemaker knows now come from the **same
source**, so they always agree with each other — and with the live platform.

### While we were at it: the current $SELF, clearly explained

If you've been meaning to catch up on how $SELF works now, the new docs lay it
out plainly:

- **$SELF is soulbound** — you earn it by participating, and you can never buy,
  sell, or send it. You spend it by burning it for marketplace items and event
  tickets.
- **It fades gently** over roughly two years, so it always reflects your *recent*
  participation — and what fades isn't lost, it becomes permanent **Tenure**
  standing (Curious → Resident → Mainstay → Pillar → Elder).
- **$SYSTEM** is still our transferable, dollar-backed community currency.

In one line, $SELF is what it's always been:

> **Verified human time, recorded where it was spent, that fades unless renewed
> — and it never becomes money.**

### Why this matters

A community of communities only works if newcomers can find their footing and
long-time members can keep up as things evolve. Clearer docs and a guide that
actually knows the platform are how we lower the barrier to participating fully —
which is, in the end, what the Eight Dignities are about.

Come ask Sensemaker something, or browse the new docs — and tell us what's still
unclear, so we can keep making it better.

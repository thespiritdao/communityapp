## We changed how $SELF ages — for the better

When we launched **$SELF**, we promised something simple: it would reflect your
*recent* participation, fading gently unless you keep showing up. This week we
made that promise truer.

Here's what changed, in plain terms.

### Before: a cliff. Now: a gentle slope.

The first version of $SELF worked like a cliff. Your contribution counted at
full value for a couple of seasons — and then, all at once, it dropped off.
That was a blunt way to measure something as alive as community participation.

We've replaced the cliff with a **slow, even fade**. Now your $SELF loses value
a little at a time, season by season, over roughly two years — instead of
vanishing in one step. Contribute this season and it counts fully. Step away for
a while and it eases off gradually, the way memory actually works. Come back, and
fresh participation tops you right back up.

### What this means for you

- **Nothing disappears overnight.** Your standing reflects the natural rhythm of
  showing up, not an arbitrary expiry date.
- **Recent effort always shines brightest** — but past effort still echoes, only
  softening over time rather than being erased.
- **Your lifetime Tenure is untouched.** As always, your permanent record —
  Curious, Resident, Mainstay, Pillar, Elder — only ever grows. The gentle fade
  applies to your *current, spendable* $SELF, never to the tenure you've earned.
- **Still gasless, still soulbound, still yours.** No fees, can't be bought or
  sold, bound only to you. None of that changed.

### Why we did it

We want $SELF to feel like a living reflection of contribution — warm and
forgiving, not a stopwatch that buzzes and resets. A slow fade rewards consistent
presence, welcomes people back without penalty, and keeps the focus where it
belongs: on participation, not timing.

In one line, $SELF is still exactly what it always was:

> **Verified human time, recorded where it was spent, that fades unless renewed
> — and it never becomes money.**

We just made the *fading* part as gentle as it was always meant to be.

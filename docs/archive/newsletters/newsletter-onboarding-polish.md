# Newsletter — A Smoother Welcome

*Draft. High-level, plain-language, member-facing. Companion to the
`newsletter-*` series.*

---

## We smoothed out the first few minutes

The most important moments in any community are the first ones — creating your
wallet, signing in, setting up your profile, finding your footing. This week we
went through that opening experience step by step and cleared away the small
snags that were getting in people's way.

None of these are flashy. All of them are the difference between "this just
works" and "why won't this work?" — and that difference matters most for the
people just arriving.

### Installing the app

Adding SpiritDAO to your phone's home screen is now reliable. The install button
behaves the first time you tap it (no more tapping and waiting), and — for those
who noticed it — the app icon now appears with its proper background instead of a
black square. If you installed earlier and still see the old icon, removing and
re-adding the app to your home screen will refresh it.

### Signing the Terms of Service

On some phones, the **Accept** button on the Terms of Service screen was sliding
off the bottom of the display, leaving people unable to finish signing in. The
screen now sizes itself correctly on every device, so the button is always within
reach.

### Setting up your profile

Your **date of birth is optional** — and now it truly behaves that way. Leaving it
blank no longer blocks your profile from saving. Fill it in if you'd like, skip it
if you'd rather; either way your profile saves cleanly.

### Creating a pod

Starting a pod is more flexible. Communities no longer need a fixed number of
co-leaders to get going — a single founder can stand one up and grow from there.
We'd rather you start the work and gather your people than wait to meet a quota.

### Why this matters

A community of communities lives or dies on how easy it is to *enter* it. Every
one of these fixes removes a moment of friction between someone's curiosity and
their first real act of participation. That's not housekeeping — it's hospitality.

If you hit a rough edge anywhere in SpiritDAO, tell us. The fastest way for us to
make the welcome warmer is to hear where it isn't.

## Cleaning up how $SELF appears across the app

When we redesigned $SELF into its current form (soulbound, earned, time-fading,
and tracked as Tenure), a few corners of the app were still reading it the old
way. Nothing was broken in how $SELF actually works, but in a handful of places
the numbers on screen came from the retired version of the token. We went through
and set them right.

Here is what changed.

### The Pulse feed now reflects the real $SELF

The community activity feed (Pulse) had a $SELF figure that was pulled from the
old token, which no longer means anything now that $SELF is soulbound and fades
over time. We replaced it with something honest and meaningful: the total
participation hours the community has earned as $SELF. It reflects real time
contributed, which is exactly what $SELF is meant to measure.

### Gifting is for $SYSTEM, not $SELF

You may have noticed a $SELF option when sending a gift or funding someone. That
was a leftover from the old model. $SELF is soulbound: it is earned through your
own participation and it cannot be transferred to anyone else. So we removed
$SELF from the gift and funding screens. You can still gift and send **$SYSTEM**,
which is the transferable currency, exactly as before. If you want to recognize
someone's contribution, $SYSTEM is the way to send value directly.

### Treasuries and balances are consistent

We also tidied up a few behind-the-scenes spots (pod treasury settings and token
labels) that still referenced the old $SELF. Everywhere you see a $SELF balance
now shows your real, current, decay-aware amount, the same number the app uses
when you spend it.

### Why this matters

$SELF is a record of your verified time in the community, so it has to be shown
accurately wherever it appears. Small display inconsistencies erode trust in the
number, and this is a number we want you to trust completely. As always, $SELF is
what it has always been:

> Verified human time, recorded where it was spent, that fades unless renewed, and
> it never becomes money.

If you spot anything about your $SELF that looks off, tell us. Getting this right
matters to us.

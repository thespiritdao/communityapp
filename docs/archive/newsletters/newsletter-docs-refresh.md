## We rewrote the manual

SpiritDAO has grown quickly, and our explanations had not fully kept pace. So we
went through the whole library of documentation, top to bottom, and brought it in
line with how the platform actually works today. If you have ever been unsure how
a token, a role, or a feature works, the answers are now clearer, more accurate,
and in one place.

Here is what changed.

### A clearer sense of what SpiritDAO is

We refined how we describe ourselves: SpiritDAO is federated infrastructure for
self-sovereign communities. In plain terms, we give any community the tools to
own its data, its governance, and its treasury, without depending on a platform
it doesn't control. And we are in open enrollment, so you can join an existing
community or start your own.

### The token model, explained properly

The parts people ask about most now read the way they should:

- **$SYSTEM** is the fiat-bound currency for work, operations, the marketplace,
  and events. Each one is backed by a dollar.
- **$SELF** is the time-bound currency. You earn it by participating, it fades
  over time, and its lasting record becomes your **Tenure**.
- **Advocate** governance holders no longer have to take any extra step. Your
  voting power is active the moment you receive the token.
- **Accreditations** are your reputation, a verifiable, portable record of your
  participation that can open doors across the ecosystem.

### Pods, described as they really are

Pods can run indefinitely or for a fixed season, and they are built from modules
in three kinds: the core tools every pod gets, optional add-ons, and premium
paid modules like a dedicated community AI agent. The docs now lay out each one.

### Sensemaker got the update too

Sensemaker, our in-app and website assistant, was taught everything above. It now
answers questions about the current platform accurately, whether you are asking
how to join, what $SELF is, or how governance works. Ask it anything at
[the SensemakerAI page](https://spiritdao.org/sensemakerai.html), or browse the
refreshed docs directly.

### Why documentation matters

A community of communities lives or dies on whether people can understand it well
enough to participate fully. Clear, honest documentation is how a newcomer finds
their footing and how a long-time member keeps up as things evolve. This was a
quiet, unglamorous effort, and it is exactly the kind of groundwork that makes
everything else easier to reach.

If something in the docs is still unclear, tell us. That is how they keep getting
better.

## Reaching each other, without handing out inboxes

As we welcome new communities, we took a careful look at how members reach one
another, and we tightened it up. The short version: contacting a fellow member is
now simpler, and your email address stays private.

Here is what changed.

### Messaging is the way to connect

If you want to reach another member, open their profile and tap **Message**. It
starts an in-app Direct Message: private, real time, and it lands as a
notification so they actually see it. No hunting for an email address, no leaving
the app, and it works the same on your phone or your desktop.

We removed the old email link from profiles in the process. It was awkward at
best, and it pointed the wrong direction anyway. Direct Messages are simply the
better way to talk to someone in the community.

### Your email is private

Your email is how you sign in and how we send you the notifications you have asked
for. It is not something we share with other members, and it is no longer visible
to them on your profile or in the Members directory. If someone wants to reach
you, they message you in the app, and you decide how to respond.

### A quick note on "Openness to Connect"

While we were here, we added a short explanation to the **Openness to Connect**
setting on your profile. Setting yourself to Actively Collaborating, Open, or
Low/Limited changes the badge on your profile and where you appear in the Members
directory (people who are actively collaborating show up first). It is a signal
of your availability. It does not lock anyone out: any member can still send you a
Direct Message, and you are always in control of the conversation.

### Why we care about this

A community platform holds a lot of trust. Part of earning it is being careful
with the small things, like making sure your contact details are yours to share
on your terms, not something handed out by default. As more people join us, that
care matters more, not less.

If anything about your privacy or your profile is unclear, ask us. We would rather
explain it twice than leave you guessing.

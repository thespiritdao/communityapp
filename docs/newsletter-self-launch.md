# Newsletter — Introducing $SELF

*Draft. High-level, plain-language, member-facing.*

---

## Your time, finally counted

We've just shipped something we've been dreaming about for a long time: **$SELF**
— a way to recognize the time and care you pour into this community.

Every time you show up — attend an event, complete a bounty, take part in
governance, serve in a role — you're building something real. Until now, that
contribution mostly lived in memory. $SELF changes that. It's a quiet,
permanent record of **verified human time**, earned wherever you spend it.

### What makes $SELF different

It isn't money, and it isn't meant to be. You can't buy it, sell it, or trade it
— it's bound to *you*, earned only by actually participating. That's the whole
point: it measures contribution, not capital. No one can shortcut their way in.

A few things we love about how it works:

- **You earn it by being here.** Events, bounties, votes, service — real
  participation, automatically recognized.
- **It's gasless.** Like everything on the platform, there are no fees to you,
  ever.
- **It grows your standing over time.** As your lifetime contribution adds up,
  you move through tenure bands — from **Curious**, to **Resident**, to
  **Mainstay**, to **Pillar**, and eventually **Elder** for our longest-tenured
  members. You'll see your band right on your profile.
- **It's seasonal.** $SELF is time-bound — it reflects *recent* participation,
  fading gently unless renewed. But your lifetime tenure never goes backward.
  Show up over the years, and the record shows it.

### Why we built it this way

We wanted a currency of contribution that could never become a game of who has
the most money. Something that honors the people who *build* — across every
community in the ecosystem — and that travels with you as a genuine reflection
of your participation.

In one line, that's what $SELF is:

> **Verified human time, recorded where it was spent, that fades unless
> renewed — and it never becomes money.**

### What's next

This is the foundation. Soon you'll be able to redeem $SELF for community
perks — exclusive merch, gated events, and more — with the people who showed up
getting first access. And as more communities join the ecosystem, your
participation will be recognized across all of them.

For now: keep showing up. It counts now — literally.

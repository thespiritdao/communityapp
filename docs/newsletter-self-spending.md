## Your $SELF now shows up — and spends — the way it should

A quick update on two improvements to how **$SELF** works across the platform.

### 1. Your $SELF balance is now accurate everywhere

Your profile now shows your **current, spendable $SELF** — the live amount you
can actually use, reflecting the gentle season-by-season fade we introduced
recently. No more stale numbers: what you see is exactly what you can spend
today.

Your **Tenure** standing (Curious → Elder) sits right alongside it, unchanged
and permanent — that's your lifetime record of showing up, and it never goes
down. The two together tell the whole story: *what you've earned over time*, and
*what you have to spend right now*.

### 2. You can spend $SELF in the marketplace again

The marketplace now accepts your $SELF the way it was meant to. Pick **$SELF**
at checkout and your contribution is burned to pay — gasless, like everything
else. Before anything happens, a confirmation screen shows you exactly what will
be spent, season by season, so there are no surprises.

A couple of things we built in:

- **Freshest first.** When you pay, your most recent $SELF is spent first — the
  $SELF that's held its value best — so you always get the most out of what
  you've earned.
- **Recognition across communities.** Down the road, as more communities join
  the ecosystem, you'll be able to spend $SELF you earned *elsewhere* — honored
  at each community's chosen recognition rate. If that ever applies, the checkout
  screen will show you the rate up front, clearly, before you confirm.

### The throughline

$SELF is still exactly what it's always been:

> **Verified human time, recorded where it was spent, that fades unless renewed
> — and it never becomes money.**

These changes just make sure that when you *do* spend it, the experience is
honest and clear: you see what you have, you see what it costs, and you decide.

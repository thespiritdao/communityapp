# Newsletter — Paying for Events with $SELF

*Draft. High-level, plain-language, member-facing. Companion to
`newsletter-self-spending.md` and `newsletter-self-decay.md`.*

---

## You can now spend $SELF on event tickets

Another place your participation pays off: **events**.

When an event has a $SELF ticket price, you can now register by spending the
$SELF you've earned — the same way you can spend it in the marketplace. Pick the
$SELF option, confirm, and you're in. Gasless, as always.

### What's new

- **$SELF tickets, done right.** Now that $SELF is soulbound — bound to you,
  never traded — paying for a ticket works by *spending* your earned $SELF
  directly, not moving a token around. Show up, earn $SELF, use it to attend the
  next thing.
- **Freshest $SELF is used first**, so your participation goes as far as it can.
- **A couple of honest notes:** spending $SELF is final — like the marketplace,
  a $SELF ticket isn't refundable if you change your mind (if an event is
  cancelled, any $SYSTEM you paid is refunded; $SELF is not, because it's already
  been spent). And $SELF spent on a ticket is retired, not routed to a pod.

### Your attendance still counts — permanently

None of this touches the best part: when you **check in and complete an event**,
you still receive your **Event Completion Certificate** — a verifiable record of
your participation that lives on your Identity page under **Accreditations**.
It's your growing, portable résumé of showing up across the community, and it's
yours for good.

### The throughline

$SELF remains exactly what it's always been:

> **Verified human time, recorded where it was spent, that fades unless renewed
> — and it never becomes money.**

Now "where it was spent" includes the events that bring us together.

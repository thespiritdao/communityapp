## Making space for bigger communities

Our first partner communities are settling in, and their feedback is already
making the platform better. This update is a direct result of building alongside
TreeGens, a community of over one hundred tree-planting members joining the
ecosystem.

Here is what changed.

### Communities can grow past one hundred members

Pods previously capped membership at one hundred people. That was fine for our
early days and quietly limiting for anyone bigger. The cap is now effectively
gone: pods can grow to ten thousand members, and existing communities have been
raised as well. If your community is large, SpiritDAO is ready for it.

### Community tokens, visible in the treasury

Communities that bring their own token can now see it where it belongs. A pod's
treasury view can display the community's own token balance alongside $SYSTEM.
TreeGens is the first: their $TGN token now appears in their pod treasury.

A deliberate boundary: community tokens are for **governance and identity only**.
They are displayed, and they shape eligibility and voting within that community's
space, but they are never connected to the marketplace, bounties, or payments.
Each community's token stays what that community made it to be.

This is built to be reusable. As more communities join with their own tokens,
turning this on for them is a matter of configuration, not new engineering.

### A fresh look for the Advocate token

The Advocate token, the credential earned through your first donation that powers
DAO-wide voting, has new artwork on your identity page. Same meaning, better
face.

### Why this matters

A community of communities has to flex to the communities that actually show up:
their size, their tokens, their ways of organizing. The best infrastructure
disappears into the background and lets a community be itself. That is the
standard we are building toward, one piece of feedback at a time.

If your community is considering joining, or your pod is bumping against an edge
we have not smoothed yet, tell us. TreeGens did, and the platform is better for
it.

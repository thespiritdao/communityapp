# SpiritDAO Update — Invitations, Streamlined

*Draft for Paragraph. 2026-07-23. A short follow-up to the July update — the
invite tools got a real upgrade this week.*

---

Hello friends,

Last update we introduced invitations. This week we took the feedback and made them
work the way they should have all along — simpler, and actually able to bring
someone into your **pod**, not just the app.

## One invite panel, two kinds of invite

Everything now lives in a single **Invite Friends** panel on your **Identity** page.
No more separate "invite code" and "invite friends" boxes — they're merged.

At the top, a dropdown asks *who you're inviting them to*:

- **SpiritDAO (app access only)** — your personal link. Anyone can copy it or email
  it to bring a friend into the ecosystem.
- **A pod you lead** — pick one, and the link becomes a **pod-enroll link**. When
  your invitee signs up, they **join that pod automatically** — no application, no
  approval step. A note right on the panel confirms *"This link auto-joins invitees
  to [pod]."*

Copy the link with one button, or drop in a few email addresses and send it directly
— the email carries whichever link matches your selection.

This closes the gap from before: previously "invite as a member of [pod]" only
*mentioned* the pod in the email; now it genuinely enrolls them.

## Leaders get pulled in at the right moment

When someone joins a pod through a link, they're a working member instantly — chat,
forum, events, and knowledge base all unlock. Their on-chain member role is finalized
in a quick second step by a pod leader.

The new part: **leaders now get notified the moment someone joins**, with a link
straight to where they finalize it (Manage → Members → Invite Links). No more
remembering to check a queue. That panel now lists each new member **by name**, and
the button simply reads **Mint Roles** — one click, one free transaction, everyone's
role finalized.

## A few quiet fixes along the way

- Pod roles now display with their community's name everywhere — *"Treegens DAO
  Member"* instead of a bare *"Member."*
- Fixed a case where a pod with a member role could still be told it had none.

As always: nothing here changes how membership *works* — one person, one vote, and an
invitation is a welcome, not a bypass (eligibility requirements like a community-token
minimum are still checked). We just made the welcome smoother.

— The SpiritDAO Team

## What brings you here?

The next time you open Home, the app asks one question: what brings you
here? Pick one of three doors.

- **Join a community.** Find a pod and the people in it.
- **Found a community.** Start a pod and invite others.
- **Just explore.** Look around first, decide later.

If you arrived through a pod's invite link, the app already knows and skips
the question. If you're not sure yet, "Ask me later" puts it off until your
next visit.

## A checklist that keeps up with you

Your answer becomes a short checklist at the top of Home, five steps for the
path you chose. Steps tick themselves off as you do real things: add your
name and photo, say what you're seeking, meet the members, join or create a
pod, say hello in General chat. Nothing to mark done by hand.

- **Show me** on any step lights up the matching icon on the home screen so
  you know where to go.
- The **next step** is always the green button at the bottom of the card.
- **Change path** swaps the checklist if your plans change. The same setting
  is under "Your path" in Edit Profile.
- **Hide** tucks the card away if you'd rather find your own way.

The Join and Found checklists disappear on their own when you've done every
step. Explore ends with a standing invitation to pick a path when you're
ready. A compact copy of the card sits on your Identity page, so you can
watch the profile steps flip as you fill things in.

Members who joined before this week will see the question once, on their
next visit to Home. There's no wrong answer, and nothing you had is changed
by it.

## People first in the Members directory

The Members directory, each pod's member list, and the pod management panel
now put people with completed profiles at the top: a name, a photo, and a
bio each count. Anonymous addresses still appear, further down. If you've
been meaning to add a photo or a line about yourself, this is a good week
to do it.

## Your events are back

Since late September, the **My Events** tab and the **Calendar** tab on the
Events page have shown nothing for everyone, even members registered for
upcoming gatherings. The cause was a permission that a security change
removed and didn't restore. It's fixed: the registrations you made were
always recorded, and they're visible again. We also changed how the page
loads so that if one part fails in future, your registered events still
show.

The same fix restored a few token-gated Forum categories that had quietly
dropped out of view.

## A tour you can finish

The guided tour's pop-up could slide under the bottom bar on shorter
phones, hiding the Finish button. It now measures itself and stays clear of
the bar, on every step.

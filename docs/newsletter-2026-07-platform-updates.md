Hello friends,

July has been our biggest building month yet. The theme, looking back at all of it: **making it easier to bring people in** — into SpiritDAO, into your pod, into understanding how everything works.

## Growing the circle: invitations are here

Two new ways to bring people into the ecosystem:

**Invite by email.** From your Identity page you'll find a new **Invite Friends** section. Enter an email address — or a whole list — and we send a personal invitation on your behalf: *"[You] from [your pod] is inviting you to join SpiritDAO,"* with a link that carries your referral automatically. No codes to copy, nothing for them to type. When they click, the door is already open.

**Pod invite links.** Pod leaders can now create shareable invite links (Manage → Members → Invite Links) with expiration dates and use limits. Anyone who accepts one **becomes a member of that pod on the spot** — chat, forum, events, and knowledge base unlock the moment they arrive. If a pod has membership requirements (like Treegens' $TGN minimum), the link still checks them — an invitation is a welcome, not a bypass.

Together these make the path from "you should check this out" to "I'm in your pod" a single click.

## A friendlier front door

We heard from several of you that the invite-code prompt at sign-in was confusing — most people don't have a code, and it wasn't obvious you could join without one. That's been inverted: the entry screen now welcomes you with a single **Enter SpiritDAO** button, with an optional *"Have a referral code?"* link for those who do. Referral codes still work exactly as before — including for future incentives — they just no longer stand in anyone's way.

## Link your own wallet — your tokens count, wherever they live

Communities that join SpiritDAO bring their own histories with them — including, sometimes, their own tokens. Our partnership with **Treegens DAO** is the first: holding their $TGN token is part of eligibility for Treegens roles and votes on our platform.

Most Treegens members hold $TGN in their own wallets — MetaMask, Coinbase Wallet — not in the SpiritDAO wallet the app creates. Asking people to move their tokens to us was never the answer. So we built **wallet linking**: from your Identity page, connect a personal wallet and sign one free message proving it's yours. That's the whole thing. Nothing moves. We can never touch, spend, or transfer anything in your wallet — the platform only *reads* the balance. Once linked, the tokens you hold there count toward your community's eligibility and votes.

Design choices we want to be transparent about:

- **Per-community, and deliberately gated** — only SpiritDAO's Executive Pod can enable it for a pod; a community's own leaders can't quietly change what counts for their votes.
- **Governance-only** — partner tokens are never involved in bounties, the marketplace, or anything financial in the app.
- **Voting stays one person, one vote** — tokens affect *whether* you're eligible, never how much your voice weighs.

## The platform now explains itself — everywhere

We finished a full documentation pass this month, and it shows up in three places at once:

- **Sixteen written guides** now cover every part of the app — including features that never had documentation before: surveys, the encrypted password vault, pod AI agents, chat and direct messages, pod agreements, and a plain-language explainer of **$SELF and Tenure** (how participation is earned, why it fades, and why your lifetime standing only ever grows).
- All sixteen live in the **in-app Knowledge Base**, alongside our video library — reorganized and easier to browse.
- **Sensemaker**, our community AI, has been retrained on all of it — and the same guides are now published at [spiritdao.org/docs](https://spiritdao.org/docs/).

One source of truth, three doors in. When we update a guide, everything updates together.

## 📅 Schedule change: evening gatherings

Our recurring **First Contact** (Wednesdays) and **Feedback Loops** (Fridays) events have moved from noon to **8:00 PM Eastern**, joining Town Hall in the evening. Every remaining session on the calendar has been updated — if you registered for an upcoming one, note the new time.

## Under the hood

The less glamorous work that makes everything steadier:

- **Newsletter delivery fixed** — a configuration bug was silently keeping new members off this very mailing list. It's fixed, and everyone affected has been added. (If this is the first update you're receiving: welcome, this is what they look like.)
- **Onboarding is smoother** — a batch of first-run fixes from watching real new members join.
- **Permissions got a hard look** — we unified how the app decides who can manage what and fixed several inconsistencies, including profile badges that displayed accreditations members didn't actually hold.
- **$SELF displays are accurate everywhere** following the token's redesign.
- **Pods scale further** — member limits raised well beyond our current size, ready for the communities ahead.

## What's next

Testing wallet linking and pod invite links live with Treegens members, and continuing to prepare the platform for the communities lining up behind them. If you hit anything odd — or anything delightful — tell us in the forum.

## Your account, fully in your hands

Communities are built on trust, and trust includes the freedom to leave.
This week we added a small feature we hope you never use: **account
deletion**, right in the app.

At the very bottom of your **Identity** page there's now a quietly
tucked-away **Delete Account** button. If the day comes, it's a
type-to-confirm step — no support ticket, no hunting for an email address,
no dark patterns making you click through five "are you sure"s. You'll get
an email confirming the request, and everything is completed within 30
days. There's also a public page at
[app.spiritdao.org/identity/account-deletion](https://app.spiritdao.org/identity/account-deletion)
spelling out exactly what gets deleted and what doesn't.

Worth being straight about the "what doesn't": anything on the blockchain —
wallet activity, token holdings, votes — lives on a permanent public ledger
that nobody, including us, can rewrite. That permanence is a feature when
it's your community's governance record, and we think you deserve plain
language about it when it's your own data. Forum posts may be anonymized
rather than removed so community conversations stay coherent.

Why now? This is part of getting the SpiritDAO Community app ready for the
**Google Play Store** — Android members will soon be able to install us the
normal way, and Google (reasonably!) requires that apps give people a real
way out. We'd have built it anyway: an organization asking for your
participation should never make leaving the hard part.

One person, one vote — and every person free to go. That's the deal.

— The SpiritDAO Team

    │
    ▼

    │
    ▼

## Demo Highlights

- **Familiar payment flow** — standard Stripe card processing, no crypto knowledge needed
- **Immediate token reward** — $SYSTEM sent automatically after payment
- **Transparent** — transaction hash provided for on-chain verification
- **Supports the mission** — donation directly funds DAO programs and community

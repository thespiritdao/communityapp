# Flow 03 — Pods: Discovery & Joining

## Who This Is For
Any community member exploring or applying to join pods (working groups/communities).

---

## What Is a Pod?
A Pod is a focused community group within SpiritDAO — like a team, guild, or working group. Each pod has its own members, treasury, forum, and governance.

                │
                ▼

│  └──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘  │

                            │
                            ▼
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│               APPLICATION SUBMITTED                             │

              │                           │
              ▼                           ▼

---

## Demo Highlights

- **Discovery is open** — anyone can browse all active pods
- **Applications are simple** — agree to accountability statement, submit
- **Pod leaders review** — decentralized approval process
- **Notifications close the loop** — user knows immediately when approved

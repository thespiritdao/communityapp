# Flow 14 — Sensemaker AI

## Who This Is For
Any member who wants to explore SpiritDAO's philosophy, ask questions, or get guidance — through a conversational AI.

---

## What Is Sensemaker?
Sensemaker is SpiritDAO's built-in AI assistant. It's trained on the organization's philosophy, Temporal Naturalism, and community knowledge. Users can ask it questions by voice or text.

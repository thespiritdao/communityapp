# SpiritDAO Community Platform - Video Tutorial Framework
**Created**: January 13, 2026
**Purpose**: Structured video content creation guide for bounty tester onboarding

---

## 📋 **Framework Overview**

This framework breaks down the platform into **7 learning tracks** with **bite-sized videos** (2-5 minutes each). Each track builds progressively, allowing testers to learn at their own pace.

**Total Estimated Content**: ~45 videos, ~3 hours of content

---

## 🎯 **Tutorial Structure Philosophy**

### **3-Tier Learning Approach**

**Tier 1: Essentials** (Must-know basics)
- Getting started, core features
- Target: All users complete within 15-20 minutes

**Tier 2: Core Features** (Primary functionality)
- Pods, events, bounties, forum
- Target: Most users complete within 1 hour

**Tier 3: Advanced** (Power user features)
- Treasury management, governance, integrations
- Target: Leaders and contributors, 1-2 hours

**Format**: MP4 (H.264)
**Audio**: Clear voiceover + optional background music (low volume)
**Subtitles**: Yes (accessibility + SEO)

### **Visual Style**

- **Screen Recordings**: Show real platform interactions
- **Cursor Highlights**: Make cursor visible and highlighted
- **Zoom Effects**: Zoom into important UI elements
- **Callout Boxes**: Highlight key information
- **Transitions**: Simple fades (1-2 seconds max)
- **Branding**: SpiritDAO logo watermark (bottom right)

### **Script Structure**

Each video should follow:
1. **Hook** (5 sec): "In this video, you'll learn..."
2. **Content** (80-90%): Step-by-step demonstration
3. **Key Takeaway** (10 sec): "Remember: [key message]"
4. **CTA** (5 sec): "Next video: [title]" or "Try it now!"

### **Voiceover Tone**

- **Friendly & Welcoming**: Not corporate or robotic
- **Clear & Slow**: Allow time for viewers to follow along
- **Encouraging**: "Great job!" / "You're doing awesome!"
- **Inclusive**: "We" not "I", "You" not "Users"

# $SELF & Tenure

*Last updated: 2026-07-17*

## What $SELF is

$SELF is SpiritDAO's participation token. You can't buy it and you can't be sent it — the only way to get $SELF is to **show up and participate**. It represents verified time given to the community.

$SELF is **soulbound**: it's bound to you and can never be transferred, gifted, or sold. It leaves your wallet only one way — when *you* burn it to pay for something. (This is different from $SYSTEM, the transferable dollar-backed token used for donations, bounties, and purchases.)

![Your spendable $SELF, already adjusted for decay.](https://app.spiritdao.org/images/docs/self-tenure/01-self-holdings.png)

## Earning $SELF

$SELF is credited for verified participation, measured in hours:

- **Attending accredited events** — check in at an event and its hours are credited (capped at 8 hours per day)
- **Completing bounties** — approved bounty work earns $SELF
- **Voting in governance** — each vote earns a small time credit

You don't need to claim anything: credits are queued, reviewed (large amounts get an extra human check), and minted to your wallet in batches. Your balance appears in **Identity → Balances** as **$SELF**.

## $SELF fades — participation is a practice

$SELF is time-bound: each batch you earn slowly loses spending value over about **two years**, fading linearly until it reaches zero. Fresh participation is always worth more than old participation.

This is by design. $SELF isn't wealth to hoard — it's a living signal of *current* involvement. A member who was highly active three years ago and vanished shouldn't outweigh someone showing up every week now.

Practical upshot: **spend your $SELF while it's fresh**, and keep participating to keep your balance topped up. Your Identity page balance already reflects current spendable value.

## Spending $SELF (burn-to-pay)

$SELF can pay for **event tickets** and eligible **marketplace items**. Paying burns the tokens permanently — there are no refunds of burned $SELF.

When you pay with $SELF you'll see a confirmation showing:

- **Price** — the sticker price in $SELF
- **You burn** — the actual amount that will be burned, which can be *more* than the sticker price if the $SELF you hold is older (partly faded) — the payment burns enough to cover the price at current value
- A breakdown of which of your $SELF holdings will be burned

Review it, then **Confirm**. The wording says "burn" because that's exactly what happens — it can't be undone.

One thing spending never does: it never lowers your Tenure. Read on.

## Tenure — your permanent standing

Where $SELF fades, **Tenure** only grows. Tenure is your lifetime standing across the ecosystem, based on total hours of verified participation ever earned — spending $SELF doesn't reduce it, and neither does decay.

Find it at **Identity → Tenure**. Tap your band card to see all five bands:

| Band | Lifetime hours | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **Curious** | 0–10 | You've joined and hold Proof of Curiosity — onboarding toward first contributions |
| **Resident** | 10–50 | You dwell here — present, participating, putting in time |
| **Mainstay** | 50–250 | A reliable, load-bearing presence |
| **Pillar** | 250–600 | A structural contributor the ecosystem leans on |
| **Elder** | 600+ | The deepest roots — years of accumulated participation |

Every member with Proof of Curiosity is at least Curious, from day one.

![Your Tenure band on the Identity page.](https://app.spiritdao.org/images/docs/self-tenure/02-tenure-band.png)

## The short version

- **Earn** $SELF by participating (events, bounties, votes) — it's minted to you automatically.
- **Spend** it on tickets and marketplace items while it's fresh — it's burned to pay.
- It **fades** over ~2 years — ongoing participation, not accumulation, is what counts.
- **Tenure** is the permanent record — it only ever rises, no matter what you spend.
- You can never transfer, sell, or buy $SELF. It's yours alone, because it *is* your participation.

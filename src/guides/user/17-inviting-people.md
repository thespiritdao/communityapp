# Inviting People to SpiritDAO

*Last updated: 2026-07-23*

## One place to invite

Everything lives in a single **Invite Friends** panel on your **Identity** page. From there you can copy a link, email it, and — if you lead a pod — send an invite that automatically enrolls the person in that pod.

Two kinds of invite:

1. **App invite (anyone)** — your personal link brings someone into SpiritDAO. They land in the app; they don't join any specific pod.
2. **Pod-enroll invite (pod leaders)** — a link tied to a pod you lead. When they sign up, they **join that pod automatically** (no separate application).

Both credit you as the referrer. Nobody *needs* a code or invitation to join — the entry screen's main **Enter SpiritDAO** button works for everyone — but invitations make arriving smoother and connect newcomers to the person and community who welcomed them.

## Using the Invite Friends panel

1. Go to **Identity** (home screen → Identity card) and find **Invite Friends**.
2. **Choose who you're inviting them to:**
   - *SpiritDAO (app access only)* — your personal link. This is the default and is available to everyone.
   - *A pod you lead (auto-join)* — pods you lead appear in the dropdown. Selecting one turns the link into a pod-enroll link, and a note confirms *"This link auto-joins invitees to [pod]."*
3. **Copy the link** with the **Copy link** button — share it anywhere (text, chat, social).
4. **Or email it directly:** enter one or more addresses (separate several with commas or new lines) and click **Send invites**. The email carries whichever link matches your selection.

Each person receives an email with a personal **Get started** link. When they click it, sign-up opens with your invitation already applied — there's no code for them to type. A pod-enroll invite additionally reads *"…join [pod] on SpiritDAO."*

Things to know:

- The email text is fixed — your name and pod are filled in, but the message itself can't be edited (this keeps invitations trustworthy and consistent).
- There's a limit of about 20 emailed invitations per member per day.
- People who are already members, or who were invited in the last 30 days, are skipped automatically — the results list tells you who and why.
- Only pods you **lead** offer the auto-join option. If you're a regular member of a pod, you can still invite people to SpiritDAO (the app link), but enrolling someone into a pod is a leader action.

## Pod invite links, in depth (for pod leaders)

Whether you generate a pod-enroll link from your Identity page or from the pod's Manage page, it's the same mechanism — a shareable link that grants **pod membership on acceptance**. From the pod's Manage page you get extra controls:

1. Open your pod's **Manage** page → **Members** tab → **Invite Links** section.
2. Choose an expiration (7/30/90 days or never) and a use limit (or unlimited), then click **Create invite link**.
3. Copy the link and share it anywhere — email, chat, social.
4. Revoke a link at any time; it stops working immediately.

When someone accepts:

- **They become a pod member right away.** Chat, forum, events, surveys, and the knowledge base unlock the moment they arrive. New visitors go through normal sign-up first; existing members just get added. There's no application to approve — the invite *is* the approval.
- **Eligibility still applies.** If the pod's member role has requirements (for example, a community-token minimum), the link checks them — someone who doesn't qualify is told why. A link is an invitation, not an exemption.
- **The on-chain role comes in a second step.** Each acceptance is queued. As soon as someone joins, the pod's leaders receive a notification, and the **Invite Links** section shows *"N new members awaiting on-chain role mint,"* listing each new member by name. One click on **Mint Roles** issues every queued role token in a single free (gasless) transaction. Members can use all the everyday pod features before this step; the on-chain token matters for treasury-level features.
- Links grant **member** roles only — leadership always goes through explicit approval.

Because leaders now get notified the moment someone joins, you don't have to keep checking the queue — but it's still good practice to mint promptly so new members' roles are finalized on-chain.

## Your personal invite link

Your personal link is the same for every invite you send — it's built from your account and doesn't change. You'll find it (and the Copy button) at the top of the **Invite Friends** panel on your **Identity** page whenever "SpiritDAO (app access only)" is selected.

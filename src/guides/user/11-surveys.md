# Surveys

*Last updated: 2026-07-17*

## What surveys are

Surveys let pod leaders ask their members structured questions — satisfaction checks, planning input, quick polls — and see the results in one place. They're lighter than governance proposals: no voting thresholds, no on-chain records, just a way to gather input.

## Taking a survey (members)

1. Open your pod's page. If there's an active survey for you, a **survey banner** appears near the top (with a clipboard icon).
2. If there's one survey, click **Respond**. If there are several, the banner says how many are available — expand it and pick one.
3. The survey opens right on the pod page: title, description, and the questions.
4. Answer the questions. Required questions are marked with a red asterisk — you can't submit without them.
5. Click **Submit**. You'll see a green "Response Submitted" confirmation.

Question types you might see:

- **Single select** — pick one option
- **Multiple choice** — check all that apply
- **Free text** — write your answer
- **Rating scale** — pick a number (1–5 or 1–10)

Things to know:

- **One response per member.** After you submit, the survey shows "Already Responded" — you can't edit or resubmit.
- **Responses are attributed, not anonymous.** Your response is tied to your wallet. Survey creators can read free-text answers individually, so write what you'd be comfortable signing your name to.
- Members don't see aggregate results in the app — the pod's leaders do, and they'll typically share outcomes back with the pod.

## Creating a survey (pod leaders)

You need the **survey management** permission (leaders have it by default).

1. Open your pod's **Manage** page and go to the **Surveys** tab.
2. Click **Create Survey**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Title** (required) and an optional description
   - **Audience** — which pod(s) receive the survey; your pod is pre-selected, and each shows its member count
   - **Start / end dates** (optional) — for scheduling a response window
   - **Questions** — add as many as you need; for each, set the text, type, options (for choice questions), scale (1–5 or 1–10 for ratings), and whether it's required. Reorder with the up/down arrows.
4. Save. New surveys start as **Draft** — members can't see them yet.
5. Click **Activate** when you're ready. The survey banner appears on the pod page for your audience.
6. Click **Close** when you're done collecting responses, or **Delete** to remove the survey and all its responses (confirmation required — this can't be undone).

## Reading results (creators)

In the Surveys tab, expand a survey to see:

- Total response count
- Choice questions: option counts with bar charts
- Rating questions: the average (e.g. "4.2 / 5") plus a distribution
- Free text: every individual answer, listed

Tip: since questions can't be edited after creation, preview your draft carefully before activating. If a question is wrong, delete the survey and recreate it before anyone responds.

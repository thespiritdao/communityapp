# Video Chat & Conferencing Guide

## What is Video Chat in SpiritDAO?

SpiritDAO includes real-time video communication directly within the app, no external tools required. There are two modes:

- **Video Chat**: Direct video calls between members, accessible through pods
- **Conferencing**: Larger multi-participant video sessions for pod meetings, governance calls, community events, and retreats

Both are integrated with pod membership, so access is automatically governed by your roles and permissions.

---

## Video Chat

### What is Video Chat For?

Video chat is designed for direct, real-time communication between pod members:
- One-on-one or small group conversations
- Quick coordination calls
- Pod leader check-ins with members
- Informal community connection

### Accessing Video Chat

1. Go to the **Chat** section from the home screen or bottom navigation
2. Select a pod or contact you want to video chat with
3. Click the video camera icon to initiate a call
4. Your camera and microphone permissions will be requested
5. The other participant receives a notification to join

### During a Video Call

- **Mute/Unmute**: Toggle your microphone on or off
- **Camera On/Off**: Toggle your video feed
- **End Call**: Leave the call at any time
- Call quality adjusts automatically to your connection

### Permissions

- Video chat access is governed by pod membership
- You can only initiate video calls with members of pods you belong to
- Pod leaders can manage video chat permissions in pod settings

## Conferencing

### What is Conferencing For?

Conferencing supports larger group sessions:
- Weekly DAO operations calls
- Pod governance meetings
- Community workshops and training
- In-person retreat coordination (hybrid events)
- Onboarding sessions for new members

### Accessing a Conference

1. Go to the **Conference** feature from within a pod or from the home screen
2. Find a scheduled conference or create a new one (if you have permission)
3. Click "Join Conference" to enter the room
4. Your camera and microphone will be requested

![Quick Meeting on the pod page: start or join.](https://app.spiritdao.org/images/docs/video/01-quick-meeting.png)

### Creating a Conference

**Required:** Pod leader role or appropriate pod permission

1. Navigate to your pod's Conference section
2. Click "Create Conference"
3. Set a title and optional description
4. Choose whether it's open to all pod members or invite-only
5. Start immediately or schedule for later
6. Share the join link with participants

### Conference Controls

- **Mute/Unmute all**: Host can mute participants (useful for large calls)
- **Screen sharing**: Share your screen for presentations or demos
- **Chat**: Text chat runs alongside video for questions and links
- **Participants list**: See who is in the call
- **Record**: Hosts may record the session (participants are notified)

![In a conference: mute, camera, share, leave.](https://app.spiritdao.org/images/docs/video/02-conference-room.png)

### Conference Best Practices

**As a Host:**
- Test your audio and video before starting
- Mute participants on entry for large calls
- Use the text chat for links, questions, and notes
- Designate a co-host for large sessions

**As a Participant:**
- Mute yourself when not speaking
- Use a stable internet connection
- Have your camera on when possible, since presence builds community
- Use the raise-hand feature or text chat to ask questions

## Weekly Governance Calls

SpiritDAO holds regular community governance calls as part of its practice. These are typically:
- Held weekly for DAO members
- Open to all members regardless of pod
- Where proposals are discussed, coordinated, and reviewed
- Recorded for members who cannot attend live

To find the next governance call:
1. Check the **Events** calendar
2. Look for "DAO Operations Call" or similar recurring event
3. Register and add it to your calendar
4. Join via the conference link at the scheduled time

## Troubleshooting

### "Camera/Microphone Not Working"
- Check browser permissions: allow camera and microphone for this site
- Try a different browser (Chrome or Firefox recommended)
- Ensure no other app is using your camera
- Refresh the page and rejoin

### "Can't Join Conference"
- Verify you have the correct pod membership
- Check that the conference is still active
- Ensure your browser is up to date
- Try clearing your cache and rejoining

### "Poor Video Quality"
- Check your internet connection speed
- Close other bandwidth-heavy applications
- Try disabling your video if audio-only is sufficient
- Move closer to your WiFi router

### "Can't See Other Participants"
- Refresh the conference page
- Ask others to check their camera settings
- Ensure all participants have joined the same conference room

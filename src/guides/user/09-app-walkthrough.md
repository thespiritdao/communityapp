# App Walkthrough & Guided Tour

## New to SpiritDAO? Start Here.

Your first visit to the home screen asks one question, then gives you a short checklist for the path you chose. There is also a built-in guided tour that walks you through every section of the app in approximately 2 minutes.

---

## Your First Visit: Pick a Path

After you accept the Terms of Service, the home screen asks **What brings you here?**

- **Join a community**: find a pod and the people in it
- **Found a community**: start a pod and invite others
- **Just explore**: look around first, decide later

Pick one. If you arrived through a pod invite link, the question is skipped and you are set to *Join*. **Ask me later** hides the question until your next visit.

![The first-visit question on the home screen.](https://app.spiritdao.org/images/docs/walkthrough/03-what-brings-you-here.png)

### Your checklist

Your answer becomes a five-step checklist at the top of **Home**, and in a compact form on your **Identity** page. Steps tick themselves off as you go: adding your name and photo, visiting a section for the first time, joining or creating a pod.

- The **next step** is the green button at the bottom of the card.
- **Show me** on any step spotlights the matching icon on the home screen.
- **Change path** switches your checklist to a different path. You can also change it under **Your path** in Edit Profile.
- **Hide** removes the card. Choosing a path again brings it back.

The *Join* and *Found* checklists disappear on their own when every step is done. *Just explore* ends with **Ready to join or found a community?**, which stays until you pick a path.

![The checklist for the Just explore path, with one step done.](https://app.spiritdao.org/images/docs/walkthrough/04-your-checklist.png)

## How to Start the Guided Tour

### From the Knowledge Base

1. Go to **Knowledge Base** from the home screen
2. At the top of the page, find the pinned card: **"Take a guided tour"** (marked with a 🧭 compass icon)
3. Click it, and the tour starts immediately

### From the Home Screen

The tour button may also be accessible from your home screen or settings depending on your version of the app.

---

![Start the guided tour from the home screen.](https://app.spiritdao.org/images/docs/walkthrough/01-start-tour.png)

## What the Tour Covers

The guided tour walks through every major section of the app in order:

1. **Welcome**: The home screen overview and navigation
2. **Identity**: Your profile, wallet address, Tenure standing, accreditations, pod credentials, and token balances
3. **Artifacts**: The community marketplace; pay with $SYSTEM, or burn $SELF to pay
4. **Sensemaker**: The AI assistant for DAO support and Temporal Naturalism inquiry
5. **Chat**: Real-time messaging and video chat with pod members
6. **Forum**: Community discussions, proposal threads, and pod-specific boards
7. **Pods**: Working groups with their own governance, treasury, events, and bounties
8. **Events**: Community calendar with token-gated registration and attendance credentials
9. **Governance**: On-chain proposals, voting, and DAO-wide decisions
10. **Bounties**: Open tasks with crypto rewards, escrow payments, and submission review
11. **Knowledge Base**: Tutorials, guides, and video resources (you are here)

## Tips for New Members

### First 24 Hours
1. ✅ Answer **What brings you here?** on the home screen and follow the checklist it gives you
2. ✅ Complete your **Identity profile** (first name, photo, bio, and what you're seeking)
3. ✅ Browse the **Pods** page and find one that matches your interests
4. ✅ Watch the **Getting Started** video in the Knowledge Base
5. ✅ Attend a **Weekly Governance Call** (check Events calendar)

### First Week
1. ✅ Apply for pod membership and get approved
2. ✅ Post in the **Forum** to introduce yourself
3. ✅ Explore open **Bounties** for ways to contribute
4. ✅ Mint your **Advocate** to join DAO-wide governance (voting is active immediately)
5. ✅ Try **Sensemaker**: ask it about Temporal Naturalism or anything about the platform

### Understanding the Community
- SpiritDAO is federated infrastructure for self-sovereign communities, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit. It is open to new communities and members: you can join a community or organize your own
- It is organized around Temporal Naturalism and the Eight Dignities, which members advance by participating across the ecosystem
- Governance happens on-chain, so your votes are permanent and public
- Participation builds a verifiable record of your community contribution
- Pods are the core organizing unit, where everything meaningful happens

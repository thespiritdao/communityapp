# App Walkthrough & Guided Tour

## New to SpiritDAO? Start Here.

SpiritDAO has a built-in guided tour that walks you through every section of the app in approximately 2 minutes. It is the fastest way to understand the full platform at a glance.

---

## How to Start the Guided Tour

### From the Knowledge Base

1. Go to **Knowledge Base** from the home screen
2. At the top of the page, find the pinned card: **"Take a guided tour"** (marked with a 🧭 compass icon)
3. Click it — the tour starts immediately

### From the Home Screen

The tour button may also be accessible from your home screen or settings depending on your version of the app.

---

## What the Tour Covers

The guided tour walks through every major section of the app in order:

1. **Welcome** — The home screen overview and navigation
2. **Identity** — Your profile, wallet address, role tokens (Hats), and community standing
3. **Artifacts** — The community marketplace for buying and selling with $SELF or $SYSTEM tokens
4. **Sensemaker** — The AI assistant for DAO support and Temporal Naturalism inquiry
5. **Chat** — Real-time messaging and video chat with pod members
6. **Forum** — Community discussions, proposal threads, and pod-specific boards
7. **Pods** — Working groups with their own governance, treasury, events, and bounties
8. **Events** — Community calendar with token-gated registration and attendance credentials
9. **Governance** — On-chain proposals, voting, and DAO-wide decisions
10. **Bounties** — Open tasks with crypto rewards, escrow payments, and submission review
11. **Knowledge Base** — Tutorials, guides, and video resources (you are here)

## Tips for New Members

### First 24 Hours
1. ✅ Complete your **Identity profile** — first name, bio, and interests
2. ✅ Browse the **Pods** page and find one that matches your interests
3. ✅ Watch the **Getting Started** video in the Knowledge Base
4. ✅ Attend a **Weekly Governance Call** (check Events calendar)

### First Week
1. ✅ Apply for pod membership and get approved
2. ✅ Post in the **Forum** to introduce yourself
3. ✅ Explore open **Bounties** for ways to contribute
4. ✅ Mint your **Advocate NFT** to activate governance voting
5. ✅ Try **Sensemaker** — ask it about Temporal Naturalism or anything about the platform

### Understanding the Community
- SpiritDAO is invitation-only and family-friendly
- It is organized around Temporal Naturalism and the Eight Dignities
- Governance happens on-chain — your votes are permanent and public
- Participation builds a verifiable record of your community contribution
- Pods are the core organizing unit — everything meaningful happens within them

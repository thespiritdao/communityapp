# Journal and Places

*Last updated: 2026-09-10*

Two ways for a community to keep track of itself: a **Journal** for what you
are thinking and deciding, and **Places** for where things are.

---

## Journal: a forum category that reads like a diary

Any pod forum category can be switched to **journal mode**. Nothing changes
about who can read or write it. What changes is how it looks and feels:

- Entries are listed newest first and grouped by month.
- Each entry shows its first photo as a cover, the author, the date and a
  short excerpt.
- Writing a reply uses the formatting editor by default, so an entry can carry
  headings, lists and images.

### Turn it on (pod leaders)

1. Open your pod → **Manage** → **Forum**.
2. Create a category (for example *Envisioning Journal*) and choose
   **Journal** as its presentation, or switch an existing category with the
   **Switch to journal** button.
3. Optional: in **Manage → Modules → Custom Appearance**, rename the home
   screen's **Forum** button to **Journal** so members see that word
   everywhere (the forum page heading follows the same name).

![Manage → Forum: choose Journal as the presentation.](https://app.spiritdao.org/images/docs/journal-and-places/01-journal-category.png)

### Good habits

- Pin one entry as the living vision and update it rather than starting over.
- Use a poll inside an entry when a question needs a decision.
- If your pod runs the Community Agent with forum ingestion on, members can
  ask it what the journal says about a topic.

## Places: your community's shared map

**Places** is an add-on module a pod leader can enable under
**Manage → Modules → Add-Ons**. Once on, a **Places** section appears on the
pod's page for every member. It is **members only**: people outside the pod
never see it, and images on it are served only to members.

![The Places section on a pod page.](https://app.spiritdao.org/images/docs/journal-and-places/02-places-map.png)

### What a place is

A pin with a name, a kind, a status, a description, an optional photo, and
links to journal entries, people and other places.

| Kind | Use it for |
|---|---|
| Asset | Land, buildings, tools, equipment the community holds or stewards |
| Partner | Organisations and allies you work with |
| Site | Gathering places, venues, meeting points |
| Resource | Suppliers, services, shared infrastructure |
| Vision | Somewhere you are envisioning, not real yet |
| Other | Anything else worth a pin |

**Status** is *Existing*, *Proposed* or *Retired*. Proposed pins draw with a
dashed outline so an idea never looks like a fact. Retired pins are hidden by
default.

**Precision** is *Exact* or *Approximate*. Approximate pins show a shaded
circle instead of a point. Use it for anything sensitive.

**Connections** link a place to journal entries, to people (with a role such
as *steward*), and to other places (with a relationship such as *supplies*).
Two connected places are drawn with a dashed line between them.

![A pin's detail card: kind, status, description, connections.](https://app.spiritdao.org/images/docs/journal-and-places/03-place-detail.png)

### Who can do what

- **Every member** can open the map, switch to the list, filter by kind and
  status, and read every pin.
- **Managing pins** (add, edit, remove, rename the section) is a role
  permission called **Places Manage**. Pod leaders have it as soon as the
  module is on; they can grant it to any role under
  **Manage → Members & Roles**.

### Adding a place (managers)

1. On the pod page, in the Places section, tap **Add place**.
2. Name it, pick a kind and status.
3. Set the location by searching an address, tapping the map, or typing
   coordinates. Leave it blank to add a place you have not mapped yet.
4. Optionally add a photo (JPEG, PNG or WebP, up to 5 MB) and connections.
5. **Add place**. It appears on the map for every member and posts a line to
   Pulse.

![Add a place: search an address or tap the mini map.](https://app.spiritdao.org/images/docs/journal-and-places/04-add-place.png)

### Naming the section

Managers can rename **Places** to whatever fits your community (up to 20
characters): *Territory*, *Commons*, *Our patch*. Tap the pencil next to the
heading.

## Sharing your own location (optional)

Members can choose to appear on their pods' maps. Open **Identity →
Location**, tick **Share my location with my pods**, find your address, and
save. By default your location is stored **approximately** (to about 1 km)
and shown as a shaded circle; you can choose exact if you prefer.

Only people who share a pod with you can see it, and only while sharing is
on. Remove it any time from the same place. Nothing about your location is
ever public.

![Identity → Location: opt in, approximate by default.](https://app.spiritdao.org/images/docs/journal-and-places/06-identity-location.png)
